Important: Data is saved using localStorage on this browser/device, not to an online account. Clearing browser storage or using another device will not preserve it. The 10 sample achievements are fictional. For records to appear on another device, use Export and Import in Feature status. Independent testing must be completed by a real person. Interview feedback is rule-based and is not AI coaching.

Use StepUp on your phone

Your laptop and phone do not automatically share saved records. Download a workspace backup here, transfer the JSON file to your phone, and import it there. The same works in reverse. Importing replaces the current browser’s workspace, so export a backup first.

No account or cloud synchronisation is provided.
